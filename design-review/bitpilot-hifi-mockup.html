Stage 3. The Stage 1 findings rendered in BitPilot's own visual language: night-sky navy surfaces, Bitcoin amber for action, sat-green for anything real, JetBrains Mono for keys and words, and layered cards with a soft top-edge highlight.

Colour carries meaning. Amber means action, green means real, a warm amber border means "protect this." Nothing is coloured for decoration.
One dominant element per screen. The secret, or the confirmation, is the loudest thing. The Next button never competes with it.
Secrets and safe values look different. Public keys sit on calm surfaces; secrets sit on protected amber surfaces and stay hidden until tapped.
Friction only where it protects. A confirmation gate appears at exactly the three irreversible moments and nowhere else.
